PROFESSIONAL CLOUD ARCHITECT — LEGACY CASE STUDY (SAMPLE QUESTIONS)

Helicopter Racing League (HRL) Case Study

Company Overview
Helicopter Racing League (HRL) is a global sports league for competitive helicopter racing. HRL broadcasts live races to millions of viewers worldwide and offers a subscription service for live telemetry feeds, predictive race analytics, and merchandise.

Solution Concept
HRL wants to migrate its existing video transcoding, telemetry ingestion, and machine learning prediction services to Google Cloud. They stream high-volume telemetry from race helicopters and tracks, run real-time ML models to predict race outcomes and component wear, and serve global video streams via CDN partners.

Existing Technical Environment
- Video encoding and transcoding jobs run on Compute Engine VMs, with frequent idle VM sprawl after race events.
- Telemetry and race logs are stored across fragmented databases.
- Predictive ML models are trained offline and lack explainability for commentators and race engineers.

Business Requirements
- Expand global fan engagement with real-time telemetry visualizations and explainable AI race predictions.
- Securely process viewer credit card payments and season ticket subscriptions with minimal PCI DSS compliance scope.
- Reduce cloud infrastructure waste from idle video transcoding and batch VMs.
- Partner with global CDNs (including Fastly) to deliver low-latency video and API responses in new regions such as Cape Town.

Technical Requirements
- Implement a custom payment card tokenization vault with deterministic encryption, duplicate detection, low latency, and annual key rotation.
- Restrict external HTTP(S) load balancer ingress to authorized CDN partner IP lists using Cloud Armor preconfigured expressions.
- Store petabyte-scale race telemetry partitioned by season in BigQuery for historical ML training.
- Use Vertex AI Explainable AI to surface feature attributions for race predictions.
- Automate weekly security and penetration testing functions triggered by deployment events via Pub/Sub.
